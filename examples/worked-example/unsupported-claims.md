# Unsupported Claim Report

Rule #4: every factual claim must be **supported**, **marked uncertain**, or
**removed**. This report exists so that the third option is never chosen silently.

## Claims withheld

### clm-00000002 - withheld

> "Intervention X causes improved outcomes in older adults generally."

**Epistemic type at close:** `unverified_claim`
**Verification status:** `fail`
**Uncertainty types:** `conflicting_evidence`, `method_uncertainty`,
`domain_transfer_risk`

**Why it was withheld.** Two independent reasons, each sufficient:

1. **Design.** The only supporting source used a cross-sectional observational
   design, which cannot distinguish causation from association. Raised as a
   `blocker` finding by the Methodologist (`rev-00000001`).
2. **Scope.** The evidence covers ages 65-74 in a single urban cohort. The claim
   covers "older adults generally". Raised as a `major` overclaim finding
   (`rev-00000002`).

**Counter-evidence.** A pre-registered replication in a rural cohort did not
reproduce the association.

**Disposition.** The claim was **not deleted**. It was reclassified, retained in
the Evidence Matrix with its full citation record, and the work's thesis was
restated as an assessment of evidential insufficiency. The reasoning is recorded in
SDL `dec-00000003`, including the dissenting view.

## Why this report matters more than the manuscript

A system that quietly dropped this claim would have produced a shorter, cleaner
document and destroyed the most informative thing about the work: **the causal
claim was tested and failed.**

That is the finding. This report is where it lives.
