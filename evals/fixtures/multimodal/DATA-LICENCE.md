# SWOS Research Grade multimodal corpus licence boundary

This directory is a governed evaluation boundary, not a synthetic image
corpus. No image bytes, invented rights statements, or fabricated review
labels may be committed here.

Before the manifest can change from `not_run` to `ready`, every asset record
must carry its source URI, rights URI or licence record, SHA-256 byte digest,
permitted purpose-specific actions, attribution statement, and required
licence statement. The evidence owner must retain the source/right records and
review identities outside the runtime output, and must verify that the exact
bytes used by each case match the recorded digest.

Permitted use must be checked separately for `view`, `analyse`, `transform`,
`create_derivative`, `quote`, `cache`, `export`, and `redistribute`. Open access
or a public URL is not itself permission for every action. Assets barred from
export must contribute only redacted metadata and limitations.

The corpus owner must document provenance, attribution, accessibility review,
mediation/capture conditions, and any derivative lineage. Human adjudication
must be independent of the provider run and must bind to the exact case and
asset digests. Removal or expiry of a right invalidates the affected cases and
leaves the evaluation `NOT_RUN` or blocked.

The repository does not grant a licence to any future corpus item. A corpus
contributor is responsible for obtaining and documenting the necessary rights
before adding content under this boundary.
