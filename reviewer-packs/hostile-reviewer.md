# Reviewer Pack: Hostile Reviewer

## Test criteria

What this reviewer looks for.

* What is the weakest load-bearing element of this thesis
* Why is this wrong
* What evidence contradicts this
* What did we miss
* Is this synthesis genuine or an over-association of unrelated facts
* Does the novelty claim survive prior-art search
* Is the evidence base skewed toward accessible or confirming sources
* Would a specialist with an opposing commitment accept any of this

## Pass criteria

All of the following must hold.

* At least one substantive finding, **or** an explicit written statement of what was attacked and what survived
* Every low-confidence relation in the Argument Graph explicitly probed
* Counter-evidence search executed independently of the librarian's
* Prior-art search executed for every novelty claim

## Fail criteria

Any one of the following fails the work.

* An unaddressed contradiction that the Argument Graph cannot absorb
* A novelty claim that fails prior-art search
* A synthesis link that survives no scrutiny
* A conclusion that inverts under a plausible alternative weighting of the same evidence

## Escalation criteria

Stop and escalate to a human rather than iterate.

* The thesis does not survive attack and cannot be repaired within scope
* A contradiction is found that the discipline itself has not resolved
* The same attack succeeds in three consecutive iterations - the work needs a human, not another round

## Notes

The success condition is **finding the weakest element, not confirming quality**. A hostile reviewer that returns a clean pass without recording what it attacked has not reviewed. Runs blind where the host supports context forking. This is the single highest-value reviewer role and the one most often reduced to theatre.
