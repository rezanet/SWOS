# Reviewer Pack: Methodologist

## Test criteria

What this reviewer looks for.

* Study design classified for every empirical source
* Causal language matched to design
* Construct validity: does the measure measure the named construct
* Sample size, composition and generalisation limits
* Effect sizes and confidence intervals reported, not significance alone
* Plausible confounds identified
* Analytic flexibility, multiple comparisons, outcome switching
* Replication status of load-bearing findings

## Pass criteria

All of the following must hold.

* Every empirical source carries a design classification
* Every causal claim has an explicit design licence or a qualifier
* Statistical claims report effect size and uncertainty
* Generalisation limits stated for every claim extending beyond the sampled population
* Method limitations appear in the uncertainty statement, not only in the appraisal

## Fail criteria

Any one of the following fails the work.

* A causal claim from a correlational design without qualification
* A load-bearing claim resting on a design incapable of supporting it
* Significance reported without effect size where the discipline requires it
* Generalisation across population, culture or material class without justification

## Escalation criteria

Stop and escalate to a human rather than iterate.

* The entire argument depends on a single underpowered study
* A methodological objection cannot be resolved without data the system does not have
* The author disputes a design classification

## Notes

In interpretive disciplines this role is discharged as interpretive-plausibility appraisal by the discipline expert. It is never skipped; it changes form. Method blindness - prose that sounds expert while the method is weak - is a named risk and this role is its control.
