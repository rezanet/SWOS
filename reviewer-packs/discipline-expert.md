# Reviewer Pack: Discipline Expert

## Test criteria

What this reviewer looks for.

* Every required analysis move for the discipline is present
* The discipline's evidence hierarchy applied, not a generic one
* The discipline's proof standard met for every claim
* Field norms observed: structure, terminology, citation convention
* Missing literature: what a specialist would expect to see cited and does not
* Discipline-specific failure modes from the pack
* For interdisciplinary work, standard levelling across the boundary

## Pass criteria

All of the following must hold.

* All required analysis moves present
* Rubric score at or above threshold on every weighted dimension
* No generic evidence hierarchy substituted for the discipline's
* No obvious canonical or recent source missing without explanation

## Fail criteria

Any one of the following fails the work.

* A required analysis move omitted
* Rubric threshold missed on any dimension
* The wrong discipline's proof standard applied
* A discipline-defining source absent from the evidence base

## Escalation criteria

Stop and escalate to a human rather than iterate.

* No discipline pack exists for the work's field
* The work spans disciplines whose standards conflict irreconcilably
* A specialist judgement is required that the pack does not encode - the tacit-judgement gap

## Notes

The **tacit judgement gap** is real and is not solved by this reviewer: experts know what matters without having formalised it. On high-value outputs, this simulated review supplements human expert review, it does not replace it. The pack encodes what has been formalised; the rest escalates.
