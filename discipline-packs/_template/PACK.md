## 1. Reasoning module

Describe what kind of reasoning this discipline actually performs. Not the topic - the *move*. Philosophy reconstructs and tests arguments; psychology appraises measurement; materials science links processing to structure to property to performance. If you cannot state the move in one sentence, the pack is not ready.

## 2. Evidence hierarchy

State the tiers of evidence **for this field**, strongest to weakest, and say explicitly where the general SWOS hierarchy is overridden and why.

## 3. Proof standard

An output in this discipline that omits a required move is incomplete regardless
of how well written it is.
