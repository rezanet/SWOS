# Discipline Pack: Art Criticism

## 1. Reasoning module

Interpretive judgement and critical voice. Criticism makes and defends an evaluative claim. Unlike art history it is licensed to judge - but the judgement must be earned through description, situated in a cultural frame, and honest about the critic's position. Criticism that describes without judging has abdicated; criticism that judges without describing has not earned it.

## 2. Evidence hierarchy

Direct encounter with the work is the strongest evidence, and its conditions - installation, lighting, sequence, duration - are part of the evidence. Documented reception is evidence about response, not about quality. Artist statements are evidence of intention, which is not the same as achievement. Market data is evidence about the market.

## 3. Proof standard

A critical claim is discharged when the evaluative judgement is traceable to described sensory and formal features, situated in a cultural and art-historical frame, weighed against the strongest opposing reading, and accompanied by an honest statement of the critic's position and the conditions of encounter.

## 4. Required analysis moves

* Provide sensory description before evaluation
* State the aesthetic claim explicitly rather than implying it through tone
* Anchor the judgement to described features
* Situate in a cultural and art-historical frame
* Address reception, including reception that disagrees
* Assess originality against genuine prior art, not against a vague sense of novelty
* Address affect: what the work does to a viewer, and on what basis
* State ethical context where the work or its production raises it
* Declare the conditions of encounter and the critic's position

An output in this discipline that omits a required move is incomplete regardless
of how well written it is.

## 5. Failure modes

* Evaluation without description: the verdict arrives unearned
* Description without evaluation: a wall label, not criticism
* Tone substituting for argument - hostility or enthusiasm doing the work of reasons
* Originality claimed without prior-art checking
* Intention read off the artist statement and treated as achievement
* Cultural frame naturalised as universal taste
* Ethical context ignored where it is material to the work

## 6. Rubric

| Dimension | Weight | Pass threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory description quality | 0.20 | Specific and evidentially useful |
| Judgement anchored to features | 0.25 | Every evaluative claim traceable |
| Cultural framing | 0.15 | Frame named, not assumed |
| Reception and counter-reading | 0.15 | Strongest opposing reading addressed |
| Originality assessment | 0.10 | Prior-art checked |
| Critical voice and positionality | 0.15 | Position and encounter conditions declared |
