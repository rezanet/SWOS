# Citation-support annotation guidelines

Annotators label the relationship between one atomic claim and one exact,
bounded source passage using only `directly_supports`, `partially_supports`,
`context_only`, `contradicts`, or `not_supported`. The source must be identified
by a stable URI and SHA-256 digest. Annotators record the exact span and may not
use publisher prestige, retrieval provider, citation count, or the model's
prediction as evidence. Mixed claims are split before annotation.

Two discipline-competent annotators label every pair independently. A third
adjudicator records the final label and rationale. Disagreement is retained.
